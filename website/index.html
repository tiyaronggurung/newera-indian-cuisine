The kitchen

Two rooms,
one long tradition.

New Era started on the Hill — 219 E Willow Grove Avenue in Chestnut Hill — with a simple idea: good food, served well. A second room followed in Plymouth Meeting. Both are quiet, comfortable places to eat, whether it is a weekday lunch or a birthday dinner.

Friends, cousins, cooks.

Owner Hemant Sen Oli runs the room; chefs Dhan Khadka and Arjun Thapa run the kitchen. The three are friends and cousins from Nepal, and the menu is the food they grew up cooking — slow curries, charcoal-grilled tikka, and the house mango dishes you will not find elsewhere.

About us
The beginning

A dream come true on the Hill

Three cousins from Nepal opened a small Indian restaurant on Willow Grove Avenue. The neighbourhood paper called it a dream come true. Six years on, we still think so.

Signature

Mango, mustard seed, cream

The New Era mango dishes started as a kitchen experiment: fresh mango folded into a light onion-tomato cream, finished with popped mustard seed. Now there are five of them.

From the tandoor

Twelve breads, one oven

Plain, garlic, cheese, Peshawari, aalu-stuffed, laccha paratha — every bread is slapped onto the clay wall of the tandoor to order and arrives at the table in under two minutes.
